# TechNest Return and Refund Policy

> **Fictional example policy:** This entire policy is invented for a college project. It is not a real store policy and should not be used for actual purchases.

## Return eligibility and window

Eligible products may be requested for return within **7 calendar days of delivery**. The item must be unused, in resalable condition, and returned with its original packaging, accessories, manuals, and proof of purchase. A return request must be approved by TechNest before the item is sent back.

## Refund processing

After an approved return reaches TechNest and passes inspection, refunds are initiated to the original payment method within **5–7 business days**. The time for the bank or payment provider to post the refund may vary. For cash-on-delivery orders, the fictional store would arrange a bank or UPI refund after confirming the customer's refund details through its official contact channels.

## Damaged or defective products

If an order arrives damaged or a product appears defective, contact TechNest within **48 hours of delivery** at **(555) 010-2020** or **hello@technest.example**. Include the order number and clear photos of the item and packaging. Keep the product and packaging until support responds. TechNest will review the report and explain the next steps, which may include inspection, replacement, repair, or a refund according to this fictional policy and the product warranty.

## Non-returnable items

For this sample policy, the following are not eligible for a change-of-mind return:

- Opened software, activation codes, and digital downloads.
- Earphones or other personal audio products once their hygiene seal is opened, unless they arrive damaged or defective.
- Products damaged after delivery through misuse, accident, liquid exposure, or unauthorized repair.
- Items missing essential parts or original packaging, unless the return is for a verified damaged or defective product.

This policy is a project example only. The separate product warranty may still apply to eligible manufacturing defects.
