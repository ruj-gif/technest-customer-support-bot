TechNest is located at **24 Market Street, Springfield**. The store is open **Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM–7:00 PM**, and closed on Sundays and public holidays.

- Phone: **(555) 010-2020**

## Shipping and delivery

We deliver to serviceable addresses in Springfield and nearby areas. Orders are usually dispatched within **1–2 business days** after payment is confirmed.

- Springfield city orders: estimated delivery in **2–4 business days** after dispatch.
- Nearby serviceable towns: estimated delivery in **4–7 business days** after dispatch.
- Delivery estimates are examples and may vary during holidays, bad weather, or courier delays.
- Any delivery charge is shown at checkout before an order is placed.

## Payment methods

The fictional store accepts **UPI, major debit and credit cards, and cash on delivery** for eligible delivery addresses. Payment options and any cash-on-delivery limit are displayed at checkout. TechNest never asks customers to share a UPI PIN, card PIN, or one-time password over a call or email.

## Product warranty

Warranty coverage depends on the product. The sample TechNest catalog lists a **12-month manufacturer warranty** for each listed item. Warranty service covers manufacturing defects under the manufacturer's terms; accidental damage, liquid damage, and normal wear are not covered. Customers should keep their invoice and contact TechNest at **(555) 010-2020** or **hello@technest.example** for help starting a warranty claim.

## Order tracking

After dispatch, TechNest sends an order confirmation and tracking details to the contact information supplied at checkout. Tracking details are usually sent within **1 business day of dispatch**. If tracking has not arrived, contact **hello@technest.example** with the order number. Never send payment credentials to request tracking help.
